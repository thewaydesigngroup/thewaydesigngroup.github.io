The Way Design Group Global

The Way Design Group's Office in Ghana serves Ghana and West Africa. We bring an approach to urbanism and place design that capitalizes on our existing rich cultural heritage and architectural sensibilities, with an added layer of innovation and know-how to position us for the global system. This includes recognizing the underlying and context-sensitive infrastructure, economics, implementation strategies and maintenance plan early in the site and urban design process.
